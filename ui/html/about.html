About

A small, multi-service chat app built to practice hosting a multi-service website end to end. A public control-plane handles login, routes each message to an internal persona service, and persists the conversation to Postgres (with a Redis cache). The app itself is just a vehicle — the connections between the services are the point.

The two personas

  • Cowboy — a warm, folksy voice from the old American West. Calls you "partner" and leans on frontier and campfire metaphors.
  • Osho — a serene meditation teacher offering gentle paradoxes about presence, awareness, silence, and letting go.

Each persona answers via a real LLM (the TrueFoundry AI Gateway) when configured, and falls back to a canned in-character line otherwise, so a reply always comes back.
